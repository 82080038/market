## 1. Konsep Dasar Behavioral Finance

### 1.1 Definisi

Behavioral finance adalah studi tentang pengaruh psikologi pada perilaku finansial dan pengaruhnya pada harga pasar. Berbeda dengan keuangan tradisional yang mengasumsikan investor rasional (Homo Economicus), behavioral finance mengakui bahwa investor **tidak selalu rasional** dan dipengaruhi oleh bias kognitif dan emosional.

### 1.2 Dua Building Blocks

1. **Limits to Arbitrage:** Sulit bagi trader rasional untuk mengoreksi dislokasi yang disebabkan oleh trader irasional
2. **Psychology:** Katalog bias sistematis yang muncul saat orang membentuk keyakinan dan membuat keputusan

### 1.3 EMH vs Behavioral Finance

| Aspek | EMH (Traditional) | Behavioral Finance |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Investor** | Rasional, memaksimalkan utilitas | Bounded rationality, biased |
| **Informasi** | Semua tersedia, diproses sempurna | Limited attention, processing errors |
| **Harga** | Mencerminkan semua informasi | Bisa mispriced untuk periode lama |
| **Arbitrage** | Mengoreksi mispricing cepat | Limits to arbitrage: risky, costly |

### 1.4 Tiga Area Aplikasi

1. **Asset Pricing:** Bias investor mempengaruhi harga (bubbles, momentum, value premium)
2. **Corporate Finance:** Manajer juga biased (overconfidence, empire building)
3. **Household Finance:** Keputusan finansial individu (underdiversification, trading too much)

## 2. Bias Kognitif (Cognitive Errors)

Cognitive errors berasal dari **faulty reasoning** — kesalahan statistik, information-processing, atau memory. Lebih mudah dikoreksi karena berbasis logika.

### 2.1 Belief Perseverance Biases

Bias yang membuat orang **mempertahankan keyakinan** meskipun ada bukti kontradiktif.

#### Conservatism Bias

- **Definisi:** Tetap pada prior belief, underweighting new evidence
- **Dampak finansial:** Lambat mengupdate view setelah informasi baru (earnings surprise, management change)
- **Mitigasi:** Bayesian updating, explicit probability revision

#### Confirmation Bias

- **Definisi:** Mencari informasi yang mendukung keyakinan, mengabaikan yang kontradiktif
- **Dampak finansial:** Hanya membaca bullish analysis untuk saham yang dimiliki, ignore bearish signals
- **Mitigasi:** Active disconfirmation — cari argumen lawan

#### Representativeness Bias

- **Definisi:** Menilai probabilitas berdasarkan kesamaan dengan stereotype, mengabaikan base rate
- **Dampak finansial:** "Saham ini seperti Amazon early days" → overpay untuk growth story
- **Mitigasi:** Base rate analysis, statistical comparison

#### Illusion of Control

- **Definisi:** Percaya dapat mengontrol outcomes yang sebenarnya random
- **Dampak finansial:** Overtrading, terlalu yakin pada strategi
- **Mitigasi:** Acknowledge randomness, track performance vs benchmark

#### Hindsight Bias

- **Definisi:** "I knew it all along" — menganggap past events were predictable
- **Dampak finansial:** Overestimate ability to predict, underestimate risk
- **Mitigasi:** Document ex-ante predictions, track accuracy

### 2.2 Information-Processing Biases

Bias yang menyebabkan informasi diproses secara salah.

#### Anchoring Bias

- **Definisi:** Terlalu bergantung pada informasi pertama (anchor) saat membuat keputusan
- **Dampak finansial:** Purchase price sebagai anchor → reluctant to sell at loss
- **Mitigasi:** Independent valuation, ignore purchase price in hold/sell decision

#### Mental Accounting

- **Definisi:** Mengkategorikan uang ke "mental accounts" berbeda (house money, safe money)
- **Dampak finansial:** Risk-seeking dengan "winnings", risk-averse dengan "capital"
- **Mitigasi:** Treat all money as fungible

#### Framing Bias

- **Definisi:** Keputusan dipengaruhi oleh cara informasi disajikan (frame)
- **Dampak finansial:** "90% success rate" terdengar lebih baik dari "10% failure rate"
- **Mitigasi:** Reframe in both positive and negative terms

#### Availability Bias

- **Definisi:** Mengoverweight informasi yang mudah diingat (recent, vivid, emotional)
- **Dampak finansial:** Overreact to recent market events, ignore long-term patterns
- **Mitigasi:** Systematic data analysis, long-term perspective

## 3. Bias Emosional (Emotional Biases)

Bias emosional berasal dari **impulse atau intuition** yang dipengaruhi perasaan. Lebih sulit dikoreksi karena berbasis emosi.

### 3.1 Loss Aversion

- **Definisi:** Loss terasa 2-2.5× lebih menyakitkan dari kepuasan gain yang setara
- **Dampak finansial:** Hold losing positions too long (hope they recover), sell winners too early
- **Mitigasi:** Pre-defined exit rules, systematic rebalancing

### 3.2 Overconfidence

- **Definisi:** Percaya diri lebih baik dari rata-rata dalam kemampuan investasi
- **Dampak finansial:** Overtrading (turnover 200%+), underperform setelah costs
- **Mitigasi:** Track performance vs benchmark, trade less

### 3.3 Self-Attribution Bias

- **Definisi:** Attribute success to skill, failure to bad luck
- **Dampak finansial:** Tidak belajar dari kesalahan, overestimate edge
- **Mitigasi:** Honest post-trade analysis, journal

### 3.4 Endowment Effect

- **Definisi:** Value aset yang dimiliki lebih tinggi dari aset yang tidak dimiliki
- **Dampak finansial:** Reluctant to sell, hold suboptimal portfolio
- **Mitigasi:** "Would I buy this today at this price?"

### 3.5 Regret Aversion

- **Definisi:** Takut membuat keputusan yang akan disesali
- **Dampak finansial:** Status quo bias, tidak mengambil risiko yang perlu
- **Mitigasi:** Framework-based decision making

### 3.6 Status Quo Bias

- **Definisi:** Preferensi untuk mempertahankan kondisi saat ini
- **Dampak finansial:** Tidak rebalance, tidak cut losses, tidak take profits
- **Mitigasi:** Automated rebalancing, scheduled reviews

## 4. Prospect Theory

### 4.1 Definisi

Dikembangkan oleh **Daniel Kahneman dan Amos Tversky** (1979). Teori tentang bagaimana orang membuat keputusan di bawah risiko dan ketidakpastian. Berbeda dari Expected Utility Theory, Prospect Theory mengakui bahwa:

1. **Reference dependence:** People evaluate outcomes relative to a reference point, tidak absolute
2. **Loss aversion:** Losses loom larger than gains
3. **Diminishing sensitivity:** Marginal value decreases dengan distance dari reference point
4. **Probability weighting:** People overweight small probabilities, underweight large ones

### 4.2 Value Function

```
    Value
      ↑
      |    Concave (risk-averse for gains)
      |   /
      |  /
      | /
      |/___________ Reference Point
      |\
      | \
      |  \
      |   \
      |    Steeper & Convex (risk-seeking for losses)
      ↓
    Losses    Gains →
```

### 4.3 Probability Weighting Function

$$w(p) = \frac{p^\gamma}{(p^\gamma + (1-p)^\gamma)^{1/\gamma}}$$

Dimana $\gamma \approx 0.65$ (estimasi Tversky & Kahneman 1992).

**Properti:**
- Overweight small probabilities (lottery tickets)
- Underweight moderate to high probabilities (insurance underpriced)
- Subcertainty: $w(p) + w(1-p) < 1$

### 4.4 Empirical Evidence

| Phenomenon | Explanation |
|------------|-------------|
| **Equity premium puzzle** | Loss aversion → demand higher premium for equity |
| **Disposition effect** | Sell winners too early (risk-averse in gains), hold losers too long (risk-seeking in losses) |
| **Lottery ticket demand** | Overweight small probability of large payoff |
| **Insurance demand** | Overweight small probability of large loss |

### 4.5 Cognitive Turn (2020s)

Riset terbaru mengaitkan elemen Prospect Theory ke **cognitive foundations** yang lebih dalam:

- **Cognitive uncertainty:** Ketidakpastian subjektif tentang keputusan yang benar
- **Memory:** Bias memori mempengaruhi reference point
- **Attention:** Limited attention mempengaruhi probability weighting
- **Complexity:** Kompleksitas masalah memperkuat cognitive uncertainty
- **Reinforcement learning:** Belajar dari reward/punishment mempengaruhi ekspektasi

## 5. Market Anomalies

Ekspresi agregat dari bias individual yang tidak dapat dijelaskan oleh keuangan tradisional.

### 5.1 Well-Documented Anomalies

| Anomali | Deskripsi | Behavioral Explanation |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Momentum** | Saham yang naik cenderung terus naik (3-12 bulan) | Underreaction to news, herding |
| **Reversal** | Saham yang naik turun dalam jangka panjang (3-5 tahun) | Overreaction, mean reversion |
| **Value premium** | Saham value outperform growth | Extrapolation bias, representativeness |
| **Size premium** | Small-cap outperform large-cap | Risk premium, neglect effect |
| **Post-earnings drift** | Sahama continue to drift setelah earnings surprise | Underreaction, anchoring |
| **Accrual anomaly** | High accruals → lower future returns | Earnings manipulation, naivety |
| **Lottery-ticket stocks** | Positively-skewed stocks underperform | Probability weighting, preference for skew |
| **Disposition effect** | Aggregate selling of winners, holding of losers | Loss aversion |

### 5.2 Calendar Effects

| Efek | Deskripsi | Evidence |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **January Effect** | Small-cap outperform di Januari | Tax-loss selling, window dressing |
| **Monday Effect** | Monday returns lower | Weekend uncertainty |
| **Turn-of-the-month** | Returns higher di akhir/awal bulan | Cash flow timing |
| **Holiday Effect** | Pre-hiday returns higher | Optimism, reduced trading |

### 5.3 Limits to Arbitrage

Mengapa anomali tidak diarbitrase habis?

| Barrier | Deskripsi |
|---------|-----------|
| **Fundamental risk** | Shorting overvalued stock tetap risky |
| **Noise trader risk** | Mispricing bisa menjadi lebih parah |
| **Implementation costs** | Shorting costs, margin requirements |
| **Horizon risk** | Arbitrageur mungkin tidak survive sampal correction |
| **Information costs** | Mencari dan menganalisis anomali membutuhkan resources |

## 6. Sentimen Pasar

### 6.1 Definisi

Sentimen pasar adalah **optimisme atau pesimisme kolektif** investor yang mempengaruhi harga di luar fundamental. Sentimen dapat:

- Mendorong harga jauh dari nilai fundamental (bubbles, crashes)
- Memprediksi return jangka pendek
- Menjadi kontrarian indicator (extreme bullish = top, extreme bearish = bottom)

### 6.2 Pengukur Sentimen

| Indikator | Data | Interpretasi |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **VIX (Fear Index)** | Implied volatility S&P 500 | Tinggi = fear, rendah = complacency |
| **Fear & Greed Index** | Multiple indicators | 0-100, extreme = contrarian signal |
| **Put/Call Ratio** | Options volume | > 1.0 = bearish, < 0.7 = bullish |
| **AAII Sentiment Survey** | Retail investor survey | Extreme bullish/bearish = contrarian |
| **Margin Debt** | Total margin balance | High = leverage risk |
| **IPO Volume** | Number of IPOs | High = market top signal |
| **Google Trends** | Search volume | "stock market" spike = interest |
| **Social Media** | Twitter/Reddit sentiment | Retail sentiment proxy |
| **Foreign Flow (ID)** | Net foreign buy/sell | Positive = bullish convention |

| **USD/IDR** | Yahoo Finance | Rupiah strengthening = bullish |

### 6.4 Indonesian NLP Lexicon

Kata-kata kunci untuk sentimen pasar saham Indonesia:

| Positif | Negatif |
|---------|---------|
| untung, laba, naik, bullish, beli, akumulasi | rugi, turun, bearish, jual, distribusi |
| dividen, bonus, split, rights, IPO | suspend, delisting, auto-reject, crash |
| lonjakan, rekor, tertinggi, optimis | anjlok, terendah, pesimis, krisis |

**Kata ambigu (perlu konteks):**

- "cut loss" → bisa positif (risk management) atau negatif (realized loss)
- "koreksi" → bisa positif (healthy correction) atau negatif (significant decline)

## 7. Herding dan Social Finance

### 7.1 Herding Behavior

Investor cenderung mengikuti tindakan investor lain, terutama:

- **Informational herding:** Mengikuti orang lain karena dianggap memiliki informasi lebih baik
- **Reputational herding:** Mengikuti konsensus untuk menjaga reputasi (analyst herding)
- **Emotional contagion:** Fear/greed menyebar melalui social interaction

### 7.2 Social Finance

Studi tentang bagaimana **struktur social interactions** mempengaruhi outcomes finansial:

- **Information diffusion:** Bagaimana informasi menyebar melalui jaringan sosial
- **Social learning:** Belajar dari orang lain (copy trading, social trading)
- **Peer effects:** Keputusan investasi dipengaruhi peer group
- **Cultural finance:** Budaya mempengaruhi risk-taking dan investment style

### 7.3 Implikasi Digital Age

- **Social media amplification:** Reddit/GameStop, Twitter/X influence
- **Copy trading:** eToro, platform copy trading
- **Influencer effect:** Financial influencers ("finfluencers") mempengaruhi retail
- **Viral information:** Information spread faster, but also misinformation

### 8.2 Sentiment Engine untuk Aplikasi

```python
class SentimentEngine:
    """Sentiment analysis engine for Indonesian market."""
    
    POSITIVE_WORDS = {
        "untung", "laba", "naik", "bullish", "beli", "akumulasi",
        "dividen", "bonus", "split", "rekor", "tertinggi", "optimis",
        "ekspansi", "akuisisi", "profit", "surplus", "lonjakan",
        "penguatan", "positif", "tumbuh", " meningkat",
    }
    
    NEGATIVE_WORDS = {
        "rugi", "turun", "bearish", "jual", "distribusi",
        "suspend", "delisting", "auto-reject", "crash", "anjlok",
        "terendah", "pesimis", "krisis", "kontraksi", "merugi",
        "defisit", "gagal", "melemah", "negatif", "penurunan",
    }
    
    AMBIGUOUS_WORDS = {"rugi", "cut loss", "koreksi", "adjustment"}
    
    def analyze(self, text):
        """Analyze sentiment of Indonesian financial text."""
        words = text.lower().split()
        
        pos = sum(1 for w in words if w in self.POSITIVE_WORDS)
        neg = sum(1 for w in words if w in self.NEGATIVE_WORDS)
        
        # Negation detection
        for i, w in enumerate(words):
            if w in ["tidak", "bukan", "jangan"] and i + 1 < len(words):
                next_word = words[i + 1]
                if next_word in self.POSITIVE_WORDS:
                    pos -= 1
                    neg += 1
                elif next_word in self.NEGATIVE_WORDS:
                    neg -= 1
                    pos += 1
        
        total = pos + neg
        if total == 0:
            return {"sentiment": 0, "label": "neutral", "pos": 0, "neg": 0}
        
        score = (pos - neg) / total
        label = "positive" if score > 0.1 else "negative" if score < -0.1 else "neutral"
        
        return {"sentiment": score, "label": label, "pos": pos, "neg": neg}
```

### 8.3 Behavioral Risk Score

```python
def behavioral_risk_score(portfolio, trading_history):
    """
    Score behavioral risk factors for an investor.
    High score = more behavioral bias risk.
    """
    score = 0
    
    # Disposition effect: selling winners vs losers
    winners_sold = [t for t in trading_history if t['pnl'] > 0]
    losers_sold = [t for t in trading_history if t['pnl'] < 0]
    if winners_sold and losers_sold:
        ratio = len(winners_sold) / (len(winners_sold) + len(losers_sold))
        if ratio > 0.7:  # selling winners much more than losers
            score += 20  # disposition effect
    
    # Overtrading: high turnover
    if len(trading_history) > 100:  # arbitrary threshold
        score += 15
    
    # Concentration: overexposure to single stock
    max_weight = max(p['weight'] for p in portfolio) if portfolio else 0
    if max_weight > 0.30:
        score += 15
    
    # Home bias: only domestic stocks
    domestic_count = sum(1 for p in portfolio if p['ticker'].endswith('.JK'))
    if domestic_count == len(portfolio) and len(portfolio) > 0:
        score += 10
    
    # Loss aversion: holding losers too long
    avg_winner_hold = avg([t['hold_days'] for t in winners_sold]) if winners_sold else 0
    avg_loser_hold = avg([t['hold_days'] for t in losers_sold]) if losers_sold else 0
    if avg_loser_hold > avg_winner_hold * 1.5:
        score += 20
    
    return min(100, score)
```

### 9.3 XAI (Explainable AI) untuk Transparansi

```python
def generate_narrative(decision, scores, reasons):
    """Generate human-readable narrative for decision."""
    action = decision['action']
    conviction = decision['conviction']
    
    narrative = f"Rekomendasi: {action} dengan conviction {conviction:.1f}. "
    
    if action == "BUY":
        strong_factors = [r for r in reasons if "STRONG" in r]
        if strong_factors:
            narrative += f"Faktor pendukung kuat: {', '.join(strong_factors)}. "
    elif action == "SELL":
        if "LOW_CONVICTION_EXIT" in reasons:
            narrative += "Exit karena conviction turun di bawah threshold. "
    elif action == "HOLD":
        narrative += "Tahan posisi, conviction tidak cukup untuk action change. "
    
    weak_factors = [r for r in reasons if "WEAK" in r or "UNAVAILABLE" in r]
    if weak_factors:
        narrative += f"Perhatian: {', '.join(weak_factors)}."
    
    return narrative
```

### 10.2 Nudge Architecture untuk Aplikasi Retail

Prinsip *nudge* (dorongan lembut) untuk membantu retail membuat keputusan lebih baik:

1. **Default options:** Default ke reksa dana indeks untuk pemula, bukan saham individual
2. **Warning labels:** "Saham ini memiliki likuiditas rendah" atau "Price spike tidak didukung fundamental"
3. **Cooling-off period:** Konfirmasi 2-step untuk beli saham dengan behavioral risk score tinggi
4. **Framing alternatif:** Tampilkan "Jika Anda berinvestasi Rp1jt/bulan selama 20 tahun..." vs "Return 15%/tahun"
5. **Loss visualization:** Tampilkan potensi loss dalam rupiah konkrit, bukan hanya persentase
6. **Peer benchmarking:** "Portofolio Anda vs IHSG vs reksa dana indeks" (tanpa leaderboard yang memicu herding)

### 11.1 Arsitektur

```python
@dataclass
class BehavioralBias:
    bias_type: str       # FOMO, LOSS_AVERSION, OVERCONFIDENCE, RECENCY, ANCHORING, MENTAL_ACCOUNTING
    severity: str        # low, medium, high
    description: str
    value: float | None
    threshold: float | None

@dataclass
class BehavioralRiskReport:
    score: float         # 0-100, higher = more behavioral risk
    biases: list[BehavioralBias]

    @property
    def has_high_risk(self) -> bool:
        return any(b.severity == "high" for b in self.biases)
```

### 11.2 Algoritma Deteksi

| Bias | Deteksi dari OHLCV | Threshold |
|------|---------------------|-----------|
| **FOMO/Herding** | Harga naik tajam + volume spike dalam 10 hari | Return > 15%, Volume > 2x median |
| **Loss Aversion** | Harga di bawah MA50 lama, volume menurun | Harga < MA50, Volume < 0.7x median |
| **Overconfidence** | Turnover tinggi (volume/market cap) | Volume/Float > 3x rata-rata |
| **Recency Bias** | Return jangka pendek tidak proporsional vs panjang | 1M return > 3x 12M return |
| **Anchoring** | Harga oscillate di sekitar level tertentu tanpa breakout | Std < 2% dari anchor, > 20 hari |
| **Mental Accounting** | Pola profit-taking (sell winners, hold losers) | Deteksi dari trade history pattern |

Behavioral risk score digunakan sebagai:
- **Pre-trade gate:** Score > 70 → warning, > 85 → block order
- **Decision Engine input:** Mengurangi conviction score jika behavioral risk tinggi
- **Portfolio monitoring:** Flag posisi yang mungkin dipengaruhi bias
