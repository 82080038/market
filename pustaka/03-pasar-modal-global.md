## 1. Lanskap Pasar Modal Global

### 1.1 Total Kapitalisasi

Per Desember 2025, total kapitalisasi pasar ekuitas global mencapai **~$148 triliun** (berdasarkan statistik WFE — World Federation of Exchanges).

Distribusi regional:

| Region | Market Cap (USD Triliun) | Pangsa |
|--------|--------------------------|--------|
| **Americas** | ~$72.6T | ~49% |
| **APAC** | ~$50.5T | ~34% |
| **EMEA** | ~$24.9T | ~17% |

### 1.2 Dominasi Amerika Serikat

- **NYSE + Nasdaq** bersama = ~$67 triliun (end-2025)
- Mewakili **~45% dari total kapitalisasi pasar global**
- AS menyumbang **74% pendanaan** perusahaan non-financial di AS (vs <30% di Eropa/Jepang)
- Rata-rata kapitalisasi perusahaan di AS: **$7 miliar** (sangat tinggi)

### 1.3 Peserta Utama

| Tipe | Deskripsi |
|------|-----------|
| **Retail investors** | Individu, akses via broker/online |
| **Institutional investors** | Pension funds, insurance, mutual funds, hedge funds, sovereign wealth funds |
| **Foreign investors** | Cross-border capital flows |
| **Market makers** | Provide liquidity |
| **HFT firms** | High-frequency trading, algorithmic |
| **Sell-side analysts** | Research, recommendations |

### 2.2 Profil Bursa Utama

#### NYSE (New York Stock Exchange)

- **Didirikan:** 1792 (Buttonwood Agreement)
- **Tipe:** Auction market (hybrid dengan electronic)
- **Karakteristik:** Perusahaan besar, established, blue chip
- **Sektor dominan:** Finance, energy, consumer goods, industrials
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** Berkshire Hathaway, JPMorgan, ExxonMobil, Coca-Cola
- **Pertumbuhan market cap (2016–2025):** +35.1%

#### Nasdaq

- **Didirikan:** 1971 (bursa elektronik pertama di dunia)
- **Tipe:** Dealer market (electronic)
- **Karakteristik:** Technology, growth, biotech, innovation
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Nvidia, Amazon, Meta, Tesla
- **Pertumbuhan market cap (2016–2025):** +189.3%
- **Signifikansi:** Pertumbuhan eksplosif didorong oleh tech giants (Magnificent 7)

#### Euronext

- **Didirikan:** 2000 (merger Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels)
- **Ekspansi:** Dublin (2018 pasca-Brexit), Oslo (2019), Milan (2021)
- **Karakteristik:** Pan-European, multi-asset
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** LVMH, TotalEnergies, Sanofi, ASML, Shell
- **Signifikansi:** Bursa terbesar Eropa pasca-Brexit, melampaui LSE

#### Japan Exchange Group (JPX/TSE)

- **Didirikan:** Tokyo Stock Exchange (1878), JPX (2013 merger with Osaka Exchange)
- **Tipe:** Hybrid (electronic + auction)
- **Karakteristik:** Perusahaan industri, teknologi, electronics
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** Toyota, Sony, Mitsubishi, SoftBank
- **Trading hours:** 09:00–15:00 JST (lunch break 11:30–12:30)

#### Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE)

- **Didirikan:** 1990
- **Tipe:** Order-driven
- **Karakteristik:** A-shares (yuan, domestik), B-shares (foreign currency)
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** Kweichow Moutai, ICBC, PetroChina
- **Akses:** Terbatas untuk investor asing (Stock Connect via HKEX)

#### London Stock Exchange (LSE)

- **Didirikan:** 1698 (salah satu bursa tertua dunia)
- **Tipe:** Electronic/Dealer
- **Karakteristik:** International, finance, commodities, global groups
- **Pangsa global:** Turun dari 13% (2000s) ke ~4% (2025)
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** Shell, AstraZeneca, HSBC, Unilever

#### Hong Kong Exchanges (HKEX)

- **Didirikan:** 1891
- **Tipe:** Order-driven
- **Karakteristik:** Gateway ke China, international listings
- **Perusahaan terkenal:** Tencent, Alibaba (secondary), AIA, HSBC
- **Stock Connect:** Jembatan ke Shanghai & Shenzhen

## 4. Perbandingan Pasar Developed vs Emerging

### 4.1 Karakteristik

| Aspek | Developed Markets | Emerging Markets |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Likuiditas** | Sangat tinggi | Moderat |
| **Volatilitas** | Lebih rendah | Lebih tinggi |
| **Disclosure** | Ketat, transparan | Berkembang |
| **Regulasi** | Matang | Evolving |
| **Foreign access** | Terbuka | Terbatas/regulated |
| **Market cap/GDP** | >100% | 30-80% |
| **Retail participation** | Tinggi (60%+ US) | Variatif |
| **Institutional dominance** | Tinggi | Berkembang |

### 4.2 Klasifikasi MSCI

| Kategori | Contoh Negara |
|----------|---------------|
| **Developed** | US, UK, Japan, Germany, France, Canada, Australia, HK, Singapore |
| **Emerging** | China, India, Brazil, Korea, Taiwan, Indonesia, Mexico, Saudi |
| **Frontier** | Vietnam, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Kenya |

### 4.3 Implikasi untuk Aplikasi

- Pasar emerging (seperti Indonesia) memiliki **volatilitas lebih tinggi** → risk management lebih kritis
- **Data quality** bisa lebih bermasalah → validasi data lebih ketat
- **Foreign flow** lebih signifikan sebagai indikator sentimen
- **Regulasi** bisa berubah lebih cepat → adaptasi cepat dibutuhkan

## 5. Globalisasi dan Interkoneksi

### 5.1 Cross-Border Listing

- **ADR (American Depositary Receipt):** Saham asing diperdagangkan di AS
- **GDR (Global Depositary Receipt):** Saham diperdagangkan di multiple bursa Eropa
- **Dual Listing:** Perusahaan tercatat di 2+ bursa (mis. BHP di ASX + LSE)

### 5.2 Stock Connect & Interconnected Markets

| Skema | Bursa Terhubung | Dampak |
|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **Shanghai-HK Stock Connect** | SSE ↔ HKEX | Akses investor internasional ke A-shares |
| **Shenzhen-HK Stock Connect** | SZSE ↔ HKEX | Ekspansi akses ke Shenzhen |
| **Bond Connect** | China interbank ↔ HK | Akses pasar obligasi China |

### 5.3 Global Capital Flows

- **Portfolio flows:** Investor institusional global mengalokasikan modal lintas bursa
- **ETF cross-border:** ETF yang melacak indeks internasional
- **Index inclusion:** Inklusi dalam indeks global (MSCI EM, FTSE EM) → capital inflows

### 5.4 Contagion Effect

Krisis di satu pasar dapat menyebar ke pasar lain:

- **2008 Financial Crisis:** Global selloff, semua bursa turun
- **2020 COVID-19:** Fastest bear market in history, global
- **2022 Rate Hikes:** Emerging markets hit hardest by capital outflows
- **Mekanisme:** Trade linkages, portfolio rebalancing, sentiment, currency

## 6. Tren Pasar Modal Global

### 6.1 Digitalisasi dan Electronification

- Semua bursa utama sekarang elektronik
- **Algorithmic trading:** >60% volume di developed markets
- **HFT (High-Frequency Trading):** <1% dari order, tapi 30-50% volume
- **AI/ML:** Increasingly used for signal generation dan execution

### 6.2 Demutualization dan For-Profit Exchanges

- Bursa berubah dari mutual/member-owned ke for-profit listed companies
- NYSE listed (2006), ASX listed (1998), HKEX listed (2000), SGX listed (2000)
- BEI masih belum listed (dimiliki oleh anggota bursa)

### 6.3 ETF Boom

- Global ETF AUM >$10 triliun (2025)
- Passive investing melampaui active management di AS
- ETF menjadi instrumen default untuk retail dan institusional

### 6.4 ESG dan Sustainable Investing

- UN Sustainable Stock Exchange (SSE) initiative
- ESG disclosure requirements
- Green bonds, sustainability-linked bonds
- BEI bergabung UN SSE initiative (2019)

### 6.5 Private Markets Growth

- Private equity, private credit, infrastructure
- AUM private markets >$15 triliun (2025)
- Less regulated, less liquid, but higher return potential
- Trend: "Private markets going public" (semi-liquid vehicles)

### 6.6 Regulatory Trends

- **T+1 settlement** (AS 2024, global trend)
- **Dodd-Frank / MiFID II / EMIR** — post-2008 reforms
- **Retail investor protection** — GameStop/AMC events → new rules
- **Crypto/digital assets** — evolving regulatory framework
- **AI regulation** — emerging

### 6.7 Fractional Shares

- US: Fully supported (Robinhood, Fidelity, etc.)
- Indonesia: Belum didukung secara resmi
- Implikasi: Akses investor retail dengan modal kecil

## 8. Siklus Pasar Global

### 8.1 Siklus Ekonomi dan Pasar Saham

```
Expansion → Peak → Contraction → Trough → Expansion → ...
    ↑           ↑        ↑            ↑
Bull market  Top    Bear market   Bottom
```

### 8.2 Historical Bear Markets

| Event | Period | Decline (S&P 500) | Trigger |
|-------|--------|--------------------|---------|
| **Great Depression** | 1929–1932 | -86% | Crash 1929 |
| **Oil Crisis** | 1973–1974 | -48% | Oil embargo |
| **Dot-com Bust** | 2000–2002 | -49% | Tech bubble |
| **Global Financial Crisis** | 2007–2009 | -57% | Subprime mortgage |
| **COVID-19** | Feb–Mar 2020 | -34% (fast) | Pandemic |
| **2022 Correction** | Jan–Oct 2022 | -25% | Rate hikes |

### 8.3 Recovery Patterns

- **V-shaped:** Fast decline, fast recovery (COVID-19 2020)
- **U-shaped:** Slow decline, slow recovery (2000-2002)
- **L-shaped:** Long stagnation (Great Depression, Japan 1990s)
- **W-shaped:** Double-dip recession

### 8.4 Seasonal Patterns

| Pola | Deskripsi |
|------|-----------|
| **Santa Rally** | Kenaikan Desember → Januari |
| **Sell in May** | "Sell in May and go away" — musim panas lemah |
| **January Effect** | Small-cap outperform di Januari |
| **October Effect** | Historis: bulan crash (1929, 1987, 2008) |

## 9. Posisi Pasar Modal Indonesia di Dunia

### 9.1 Statistik

| Metrik | Nilai | Posisi Global |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Market cap** | ~$0.8T | Top 20-25 |
| **Jumlah emiten** | ~900+ | Top 15 by count |
| **Market cap/GDP** | ~50% | Emerging market average |
| **Foreign ownership** | ~30-40% | Tinggi untuk EM |
| **Retail investors** | ~12+ juta SID | Berkembang pesat |

### 9.2 Karakteristik Unik

- **Single exchange:** BEI adalah satu-satunya bursa (tidak seperti US dengan NYSE + Nasdaq)
- **Foreign flow driven:** Sentimen sangat dipengaruhi aliran dana asing
- **Commodity-heavy:** Sektor energi, barang baku, finansial dominan
- **Retail growth:** Pertumbuhan investor retail sangat pesat (Yuk Nabung Saham)
- **Syariah market:** Pasar syariah terbesar di dunia (ISSI)

### 9.3 Tantangan

- **Likuiditas:** Banyak saham illiquid (thin trading)
- **Disclosure quality:** Variatif, beberapa emiten kurang transparan
- **Corporate governance:** Masih berkembang
- **Market manipulation:** Risiko pump-and-dump pada saham illiquid
- **Currency risk:** IDR volatility mempengaruhi return investor asing

### 9.4 Peluang

- **Demographic dividend:** Populasi muda, growing middle class
- **Digital adoption:** Tinggi, mobile trading apps booming
- **Government initiative:** Tax incentives untuk investor retail
- **ESG trend:** Growing ESG awareness dan disclosure
- **IPO pipeline:** Banyak perusahaan tech/digital plan IPO
